IRS Transcript Requests Workflow Guide
Payment Pending / Not Verified
Use this when the client clicked the Transcript Help payment button, but payment has not been verified in Stripe yet.
Paid / Needs Review
Use this after payment is confirmed. Contact the client and gather the authorization or information needed for transcript help.
Authorization Needed
Do not pull or review IRS records until proper client authorization and identity information are complete.
Completed
Use this after transcript help, tax records review, and next-step guidance have been completed or documented.
IRS transcript availability depends on IRS processing. Your office can still provide fast follow-up once authorization and available records are ready.
Written Tax Estimate Review Workflow Guide
Requested / Clicked

Payment Pending / Not Verified
Use this when the client clicked the Stripe button, but payment has not been verified yet.
Paid / Needs Written Review
Payment is confirmed and the written tax estimate review needs to be prepared.
Completed
The written tax estimate review has been completed and delivered or saved.
Stripe automation is not connected yet, so payment status is manual for now.
Balance Due Review Guide
Possible Balance Due
Client may owe federal or state tax based on the estimate. Confirm the numbers before giving final guidance.
Schedule Review
Best first step is usually a short review to explain why they may owe and what may change.
Strategy Review Option
If they want written guidance, direct them to the $29 written tax strategy review workflow.
Payment Plan / Missing Info
Look for missing credits, withholding, estimated payments, business expenses, or payment-plan needs.
Balance due estimates are not final tax returns. Review documents before giving final advice.
Refund Leads Guide
Possible Refund
Client may have a refund based on the estimate. Confirm documents before giving final numbers.
Follow Up to File
Best next step is to ask if they want help completing and filing the return.
Confirm Documents
Check W-2s, 1099s, dependents, credits, withholding, and any missing tax documents.
Convert to Tax Client
If they want filing help, move them toward client intake, secure upload, and paid tax prep.
Refund estimates are lead-generation estimates only. Final refund depends on complete tax documents.
