Office Procedure Before Pulling IRS Transcripts
1. Verify PaymentConfirm Stripe payment before doing transcript work. Then mark Payment Status as Paid / Verified.
2. Send Form 8821Payment does not authorize transcript access. Send or request the signed IRS authorization.
3. Receive Signed 8821Mark Form 8821 Status as 8821 Signed / Received and enter the received date.
4. Verify IdentityConfirm required identity information before accessing IRS records. Enter the identity verified date.
5. Confirm Transcript Type and YearsConfirm what records are needed: Wage and Income, Account, Return, Record of Account, or other records.
6. Pull in PitBullTaxOnly pull once payment, authorization, identity, transcript type, and tax years are complete.
7. Review and DeliverEnter pull date, received date, delivery method, delivery date, and final notes.
8. Complete the RequestOnce delivered, the request moves out of open work and into Completed Transcript Requests.
Automation plan: Stripe payment verification first, then authorization follow-up, then PitBullTax monitoring alerts, then client alert emails. Human review stays required before explaining IRS activity to the client.
